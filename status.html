Popups & rules

What shows where, and why nobody gets annoyed.
Open live designs

Popup 1 — ARK Melts (10% off)

Testing
Email · 10%→ Phone · 15% (optional)→ Success
  • ◎Shows only on 4 pages: Melts Extra Strong, Melts Original, NeuroFuel Kit, and the ark-melts landing page.
  • ◷Never instantly: on phones it waits for 5 seconds + a third of the page scrolled; on computers additionally for a real exit movement. An "Exit only" mode exists as a switch.
  • ▸The small "GET 10% OFF" teaser tab is not on the page at arrival — it appears only after someone closes the popup. Closing the teaser hides it for that visit. Clicking it always opens the popup.

Popup 2 — Leaving Already?

Testing
Email · 10%→ Phone · +5% (optional)→ Success
  • ◎Shows everywhere except the 4 Melts pages, cart, checkout, ad landing pages and policy pages.
  • ◷Opens only on real leaving behavior: 3+ pages viewed, 45+ seconds of actual on-screen time, then a genuine exit move (mouse to top edge on desktop / long fast scroll-up on mobile). Never a timer. No teaser, ever.

Politeness rules

  • 1Maximum one popup per visit across the whole store.
  • 2Close one popup → no other popup that visit (at most the teaser on Melts pages).
  • 3Closed = quiet for 7 days (Melts popup) / 30 days (Leaving popup).
  • 4Signed up = never sees any popup or teaser again — shared across both popups.
  • 5Cart and checkout are sacred — nothing ever interrupts a purchase.
  • 6All 9 store languages supported; phone country pre-selected per market.

Klaviyo connection

For the email team — what changes and what doesn't.
Flows untouched

What happens on signup

Popup→ Newsletter list→ PMA | Welcome Flow→ WELCOME10 email + 5% SMS series
  • ✓The popups add people to the same Newsletter list as today. Every flow, email and SMS continues exactly as it is — nothing is rebuilt.
  • ✓Email + phone are submitted together at the end of the journey, so SMS consent exists before the flow starts (today's forms submit email first, which can make the flow skip SMS #1).
  • ✓Typing an email is not consent — nothing is sent until the visitor clicks Continue and finishes. Success appears only after Klaviyo confirms receipt.
  • ✓Every profile is tagged with custom_source ("ARK Popup - Product" / "ARK Popup - Exit") and popup_signup_source (exact page) — the email team can branch flow content on these anytime, without any change on the popup side.

At switch-over (later, owner-approved)

  • 1The 3 old Klaviyo sign-up forms get unpublished — forms only; flows and tracking stay.
  • 2Klaviyo's site tracking remains active (Browse / Site Abandonment flows depend on it).
  • 3Fully reversible: re-publish the old forms anytime.
