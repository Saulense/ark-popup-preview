How these numbers work

Signup and welcome-delivery counts come from Klaviyo. An accepted form request is not proof of a new subscriber; existing subscribers may not enter the welcome flow again. Internal/test profiles are excluded from provider counts.

View tracking can be blocked. Rates are hidden for incomplete tracking periods or inconsistent counts. A day or a version with few views is not enough to declare a winner. Changes over time are associations, not proof of cause.

Orders are observed within seven days of the selected signup cohort, not necessarily placed during the selected dates. Neither marketing spend nor profit is connected, so no ROI or ROAS is calculated.

Refresh is manual, on unlock or stale tab focus. Existing sync limits, history and server-side access checks are unchanged. No background polling or marketing sends.

How the team should use this

Review both designs and each step before changing copy or timing. Record the date, version, exact change and expected result in Obsidian. Change one main variable at a time.

Compare the same weekdays and traffic sources where possible. Look at views, submission rate, new subscribers and welcome delivery together. Higher signup volume can simply mean more traffic. Use a controlled A/B test to establish causal uplift.

Version counts describe tracked sessions within the selected range. They cannot be joined to individual subscribers, devices or ad spend with this data. All versions and prior observations remain in history.
