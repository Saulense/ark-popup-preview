Data and current setup

Email and SMS counts are distinct popup subscribers, not HTTP request counts. Welcome delivery uses Klaviyo’s received-message events; this does not prove a message was read. Existing subscribers may not re-enter the welcome flow.

Internal addresses and known test accounts are excluded. Delivery and order results belong to the selected signup cohort and can update after the selected end date. Order values are kept in separate currencies and are not proof that the popup caused the sale.

Stored history is kept in Supabase. Dashboard reports cache locally and refresh when opened or when you press Refresh. Provider reads have a fixed limit, no automatic retry, and pause after three consecutive failures. No background polling or marketing sends.

Theme number 2 is live. The two custom popups use Newsletter → PMA Welcome Flow. Klaviyo tracking is enabled; the three legacy popup forms were switched off. Checkout testing was waived; the accepted offer-copy exceptions remain unchanged.
